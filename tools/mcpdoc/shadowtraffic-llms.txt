# ShadowTraffic

> ShadowTraffic is a containerized service (`shadowtraffic/shadowtraffic`) for declaratively generating synthetic data into Kafka, relational databases, cloud storage, webhooks, and other backends from a single JSON config, driven by `_gen` functions instead of a hand-written producer. Third-party commercial product — docs.shadowtraffic.io is the authoritative source, no vendor MCP/llms.txt of its own (this file is hand-maintained from the site's sitemap.xml).
