### Top priorities

1. **C1:** the audit router JSON is malformed. The FSI audit control does not work.
2. **C3:** layer 04 silently strips the layer 02 TLS 1.2/1.3 and AES-GCM pins from the Kafka CR.
3. **C2:** the runbook stages secret manifests in `/tmp`.
4. **Code paths that do not match their stated guarantees:**
   - python-consumer commits offsets after handler failures, despite claiming at-least-once.
   - python-producer drops retryable delivery errors instead of retrying, then DLQing.
   - The cp-rhel Flink role downloads a non-existent artifact (H34).
5. **Credentials and auth:**
   - SASL/PLAIN with API key literals in every python README, which conflicts with the FSI mTLS canon.
   - `curl -k -u admin:changeme` in splunk/README.md.
   - Plaintext CC secrets in the observability export templates.
   - db_connector Mongo writes the secret into `connection.user`.
