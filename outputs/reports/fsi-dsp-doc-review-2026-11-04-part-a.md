How severity was assigned. **Critical** means a doc you follow would quietly weaken security, or the doc leaks client-identifying data. **High** means a factually wrong or outdated claim, or a copy-paste command or config that fails outright (Terraform errors, CLI subcommands that don't exist, wrong connector classes). Those fail loudly, so they are High rather than Critical. **Medium** covers canon conflicts, internal inconsistencies, unverified-but-risky claims, and cross-references that don't match the repo. **Low** covers cleanliness and style.

What "verified" means. A finding marked verified was checked against a live page on docs.confluent.io, or against the Terraform registry (`confluentinc/confluent` v2.93.0). A finding marked **inference** comes from vendor knowledge outside Confluent docs (IBM, Microsoft, CockroachDB, MongoDB, OpenSSL), which the allowed tools could not check.

Other checks:
- All relative markdown links in the 35 files resolve on disk, so there are no broken links.
- Backtick path references resolve too, except where noted below.
- No credentials, account IDs, or real hostnames turned up. Every hostname uses `*.fsi.internal` or vendor placeholder patterns (`abc12345`, `adb-1234567890`, `123456789012`, `xxxxx`).
